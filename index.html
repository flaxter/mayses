Everything here streams from free public archives — nothing is hosted on this site.

  • Bobe-mayses — folktales read by Ruth Rubin (c. 1960), from the Ruth Rubin Legacy Archive at YIVO. Sourced from Y. L. Cahan's Folks-mayses.
  • Everything else — the Sami Rohr Library of Recorded Yiddish Books: native Yiddish speakers recorded at the Jewish Public Library of Montreal in the 1980s–90s, digitized by the Yiddish Book Center, streamed from the Internet Archive.
  • The Swedish sections (Småsagor, Pino, folktales, Tora, songs, Läsa med) stream directly from UR Play, Sweden's public educational broadcaster — Yiddish is an official minority language there. Videos open in a pop-up player. More on UR Play: Grannar – jiddisch (sitcom for learners), Itst yidish!, Bobes mat (cooking), Jiddisch in der velt.
  • The player remembers where you stopped in each story. Motl's chapters play one after the next, like a real audiobook.
